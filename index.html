Wine Companion

Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short
  • We keep only what the app needs: your sign-in, your cellar, your journal and the photos you save.
  • Your data is stored in the EU.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, at any time.

Who we are

Wine Companion is a small independent app made in Sweden by Michael Lindsley, who is responsible for your data (the "controller" under the GDPR). Contact: mlindsley70@gmail.com.

What we keep

  • Your account: you sign in with Apple. We receive an account id from Apple and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (often a private relay address from Apple).
  • What you save in the app: your cellar, journal notes, wish list and recently scanned wines.
  • Photos you save: one photo per wine, if you choose to keep one.
  • Scans: the text read from a label, which wine it matched and when, and how many scans you made each day (for the daily limits).

We do not collect your location, contacts or anything else from your phone, and the app has no advertising or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To recognise the wine on a label and show you its story, facts and how it will evolve.
  • To keep your cellar and journal, and show them on any phone where you sign in.
  • To keep the service fair and running: daily limits, and finding and fixing errors.

The legal basis is that we need the data to give you the service you asked for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and working (article 6(1)(f)).

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores the database and photos, on servers in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • OpenAI and Anthropic (AI services) receive the label text or label photo, and facts about the wine, to read the label, research the wine and write its story. They receive no name or email. Under their business terms, data sent this way is not used to train their models. These companies are in the USA; transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the EU's standard contractual clauses.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and, for testers, TestFlight.

We share nothing else with anyone, and we never sell data.

Wine knowledge is shared

When you scan a wine we did not know, the wine itself (producer, name, year, region, grapes) and the facts and story found for it are added to the app's shared wine knowledge, so the next person finds it at once. This is about the wine, not about you: it does not say who scanned it.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your cellar, journal, wish list, scans and photos are deleted at once. Scan records are kept without any link to you. Backups are overwritten within 7 days.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it. Delete your account from the app (Settings → Delete my account), or write to mlindsley70@gmail.com for anything else. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY.

Children

Wine Companion is about alcohol and is meant for adults (18 and over in Sweden).

Changes

If this text changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Big changes are shown in the app.
